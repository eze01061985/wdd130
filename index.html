Jujuy, Argentina

It is a province of contrasts, where the mountains look like painted by hand and the towns keep an ancient soul. The most common starting point is San Salvador de Jujuy, but the real magic begins when you go into the Quebrada de Humahuaca. It was declared World Heritage by UNESCO. This very old route was part of the Inca Trail, and each town in it has its own rhythm and charm.
